Privacy Policy.

Draft for review. The publisher, privacy contact, effective date, and production data practices are pending confirmation.

Scope

This policy describes the NOIRGAMES website and the current development build of Too Hot to Handle!.

Website visits

The pages do not include account registration, submission forms, advertising scripts, or analytics tags. The service hosting the pages may process technical request data such as IP addresses and browser information to deliver and secure the site. Any hosting sign-in is handled by the hosting service.

Game progress and settings

The current game code uses local storage to remember the highest unlocked chapter, completed and skipped chapters, and the sound preference. The game code does not send those records to a developer server. To remove the local records, clear the game’s stored data using your browser or platform controls where available. This also resets saved progress and preferences.

Platform services and ads

The local preview simulates rewarded ads. If a future TikTok release enables rewarded videos, TikTok delivers them and reports whether playback was completed so the game can award the optional benefit. TikTok handles information through its own services under the privacy terms applicable in your region. TikTok Privacy Policy (U.S.).

Updates

This page will be updated to describe the data practices that apply to the released game and identify how to contact the publisher about privacy questions.
