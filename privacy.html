Privacy Policy.

Too Hot Too Handle!
Published by RUDHNAN HABIB
Effective November 21, 2026

App and publisher

This Privacy Policy applies to Too Hot Too Handle!, a comic-style puzzle game featuring Vanessa and Nico, and its official website. The publisher and party responsible for the game’s data practices is RUDHNAN HABIB, the enterprise named on the app’s TikTok listing. The TikTok Mini Game App ID is 7709272343012657173. The game is also available as a browser build on itch.io.

Information stored by the game

The game stores the highest unlocked chapter, completed or skipped chapters, and sound preference in local storage on your device or browser. It uses this information to restore your progress and settings between visits. The game does not send these saved records to a publisher-operated server. Clearing the game’s stored data or your browser’s site data removes these records and resets your progress and preferences. Your hosting platform may provide additional storage controls.

TikTok sign-in and return rewards

When TikTok sign-in is enabled, the game obtains a temporary login code from TikTok and sends it to the publisher’s HTTPS sign-in service. That service exchanges the code with TikTok for an app-specific player identifier and issues a signed session. TikTok access and refresh tokens stay on the service during the exchange and are discarded afterward. The game keeps the session in memory until it expires or the game closes. Game progress continues to be stored on your device.

If you choose the profile sidebar or home-screen shortcut features, the game asks TikTok to start the selected flow and check whether you are eligible for a return reward. The game stores your app-specific identifier with a dated claim record and your unused free hints on your device to prevent repeat claims. Clearing the game’s local data removes those records. The sign-in service does not maintain a player database. Its hosting provider processes technical network information needed to deliver and secure the service. Privacy requests may be sent to the contact below.

Platform services and rewarded ads

When you play through TikTok, TikTok operates the platform and any available rewarded video advertisements. If you choose Hint, Complete Clue, or +30s and an ad is available, the game requests an ad from TikTok. TikTok reports whether playback completed or failed. The game uses that result to award the selected benefit once and resume play. Completing one ad grants one hint, one clue completion, or up to 30 seconds of additional time with a one-minute timer cap. The browser build uses simulated ad playback for these rewards. TikTok may process device, network, usage, and advertising information according to its own privacy policy and your platform settings. See the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Website and browser hosting

The official website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, when serving and securing these pages. The website itself does not include account registration, submission forms, advertising scripts, or analytics tags. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The browser game is hosted on itch.io, which processes information under the itch.io Privacy Policy.

Information you send for support

If you contact the publisher for help or a privacy request, the publisher receives the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and screenshots. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the reported problem. Include only information needed for the request. The publisher retains support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and international services

The game code does not provide the publisher with a list of players or transmit local game saves to the publisher. TikTok, GitHub, itch.io, and any email service used for support handle information through their own services and infrastructure, which may operate in other countries. Review the linked platform policies for their processing, sharing, retention, and international transfer practices.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can clear local game progress using your browser or platform controls, choose whether to request an optional ad reward, and use the privacy controls offered by your hosting platform. Contact the publisher about access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent for support. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law. Platform account information and advertising preferences are managed through the relevant platform.

Children and eligibility

Players must meet the age and eligibility requirements of the platform through which they access the game. The game does not request dates of birth, profiles, chat messages, photos, or precise location. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to the publisher, contact the publisher so the concern can be investigated and the information deleted where appropriate.

Policy updates

This policy is effective November 21, 2026. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes to the game’s data practices will be reflected in the policy when those changes take effect.

Contact the publisher

For support, privacy questions, or requests about your information, email RUDHNAN HABIB at noirbrandx1@gmail.com. Please include the app name Too Hot Too Handle! and enough detail to identify your request.
